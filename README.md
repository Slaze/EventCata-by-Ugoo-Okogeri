# EventCata

A personal event flyer catalogue for your phone. Snap or attach a poster, or type a simple name, time, and place, mark yourself as Going, and share the event as text. It works offline on the device. It is your pocket archive of things you care about attending, not a public party feed.

## Who it is for

People who want a calm, private list of upcoming events and flyers on their phone.

## What you can do

- Add a flyer from a photo or file, or create an event with a name, when, and where
- Open a saved event and mark yourself Going (that mark is for you, not a public crowd count)
- Share an event as text
- Reopen the catalogue later, including when you are offline
- Install it on the home screen
